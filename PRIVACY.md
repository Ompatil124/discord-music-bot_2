# Privacy Policy for ♫ DC MUSIC

**Last Updated:** November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **♫ DC MUSIC** ("we", "us", or "our", Application ID: `1533515061526663298`) collects, uses, stores, and protects data when you use our Discord bot application.

We take user privacy seriously. We only collect the minimum amount of data required to provide and operate the music playback features.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Data Stored in Our Database
When you use certain bot features, we may store the following technical identifiers in our secure database:
1. **Guild & Voice Settings:**
   - **Discord Guild (Server) ID:** To associate music settings and 24/7 channel preferences with your server.
   - **Voice & Text Channel IDs:** Saved only when 24/7 mode or specific notification channels are configured.
2. **User Playlists:**
   - **Discord User ID:** Saved when you create custom personal playlists so only you can manage your songs.
   - **Playlist Track Metadata:** Track titles, URLs, and authors that you explicitly choose to save into a playlist.

### B. What We DO NOT Collect or Store
- **NO Voice Recording or Eavesdropping:** ♫ DC MUSIC transmits audio into voice channels. It **never** listens to, monitors, records, or stores human voice audio.
- **NO Private Personal Information:** We do not collect passwords, real names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or payment information.
- **NO Message Content Storage:** We process slash commands and prefix instructions transiently in memory to execute the requested action. We do not store your chat logs or message history.

---

## 2. How We Use Collected Data
The data collected is used solely to:
- Connect the bot to your voice channel and resume playback (24/7 mode).
- Store and retrieve your custom music playlists.
- Manage bot permissions and volume levels on a per-server basis.
- Diagnose and debug technical connection issues.

We do **not** sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user data to third parties or advertisers.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services
♫ DC MUSIC interacts with third-party APIs solely to fetch audio streams and metadata:
- **Discord API:** For transmitting audio frames, slash commands, and interactive components in accordance with Discord's [Developer Policy](https://discord.com/developers/docs/legal).
- **Lavalink & Audio Providers (YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud):** To stream audio requested by users in voice channels.
- **MongoDB:** Our secure database provider used to store configuration data.

---

## 4. Data Retention & Deletion

### A. Automatic Deletion
- When the bot is removed or kicked from a server, server-specific configurations (such as 24/7 voice channel settings) are automatically cleaned up or become inert.
- When you delete a custom playlist via the playlist commands, the associated tracks and entries are permanently deleted immediately from the database.

### B. Manual Data Removal Requests
You have the right to request full deletion of all data associated with your Discord User ID or Discord Server ID at any time.

To request data deletion:
1. Contact the bot developer on Discord: `omgameryt124`
2. Open an issue on our GitHub repository: [https://github.com/Ompatil124/discord-music-bot_2](https://github.com/Ompatil124/discord-music-bot_2)

All verified data removal requests are processed within 48 hours.

---

## 5. Security of Your Data
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the integrity of our database and connection tokens:
- All database connections are encrypted over TLS/SSL.
- Access credentials and tokens are stored in strict environment variables and are never committed to public repositories.
